Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Welcome to ArcadeZone ("we," "our," or "us"), accessible at this website. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you visit our website.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

  • Usage Data: pages visited, time spent on site, games played, browser type, and device information.
  • Cookies: small data files stored on your device to improve your experience and serve relevant advertising.
  • Log Data: IP address, browser type, and access times collected automatically by our hosting provider.

2. How We Use Information

We use collected information to:

  • Operate and improve our website and game library
  • Analyze site traffic and usage patterns
  • Serve relevant advertisements through Google AdSense and other ad partners
  • Maintain site security and prevent abuse

3. Third-Party Advertising

We use Google AdSense and may use other advertising partners to display ads on our site. These third parties may use cookies, web beacons, and similar technologies to collect information about your visits to this and other websites in order to provide personalized or non-personalized ads.

You can learn more about how Google uses data by visiting Google's Privacy & Terms page.

You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info/choices.

4. Embedded Games

ArcadeZone features games hosted by third-party providers, including GameDistribution and other game publishers. These providers may have their own privacy policies and may collect data independently when you play embedded games. We encourage you to review their respective privacy policies.

5. Children's Privacy

Our site is intended for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can remove it.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information collected through our site, though no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

7. Cookie Notice and Your Choices

ArcadeZone displays a cookie notice on first visit. By using the site after seeing this notice, you consent to our use of cookies for site operation, traffic measurement, and advertising where applicable.

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect site functionality and ad relevance.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

9. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please reach out via our Contact page.
