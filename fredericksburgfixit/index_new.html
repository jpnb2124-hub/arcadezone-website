Local Fredericksburg Virginia

Fredericksburg, Va is one of America's most historic independent cities, perfectly situated on the banks of the Rappahannock River. Its identity is forged in the crucible of American history, from its roots as George Washington's boyhood home to its pivotal role as the bloody crossroads of the Civil War, commemorated today by the Fredericksburg and Spotsylvania National Military Park.
