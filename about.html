About ArcadeZone

ArcadeZone is an independent browser gaming site operated under the pxlrush.com domain. The site is maintained by a small editorial team that reviews game links, writes play guides, and keeps policy pages current.

Our focus is simple: publish useful game pages, not thin link directories. Every game that stays indexed is reviewed for control quality, replay value, and reliability on modern browsers.

What makes this site different

We do not treat every title the same. Some game pages are kept as archive references, while our curated pages include original commentary, practical strategy, and maintenance updates. This helps players find better games faster and gives search users real value.

Editorial process

Before a game is listed as indexed content, we check source reliability, basic gameplay quality, and whether we can provide unique written guidance. We also update pages when controls, links, or gameplay behavior changes.
