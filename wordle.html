Wordle

Category: puzzle • Tag: Word

Wordle is a compact deduction game where information value matters more than lucky letter hits.

How to approach this game

Open with balanced words containing common consonants and vowels, then refine quickly based on position feedback. Prioritize narrowing candidates over repeating uncertain letters.

Common mistakes to avoid

Many players chase green letters too early and ignore broader elimination. In early turns, information gain is your strongest asset.

Wordle is perfect for short daily play and language-focused puzzle fans.
