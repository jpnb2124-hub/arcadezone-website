Editorial Policy

Last updated: August 2026

ArcadeZone publishes browser game pages for users who want fast access plus useful gameplay guidance. This page explains how we choose games, write content, and maintain quality standards.

1. Selection criteria for indexed game pages

  • Reliable source link that opens and runs in major browsers
  • Gameplay quality and replay value worth recommending
  • Enough depth to support unique editorial commentary
  • No deceptive redirects or unsafe distribution behavior

2. Content standards

  • Each indexed page must include original guidance and strategy notes
  • We avoid boilerplate and do not mass-publish near-duplicate text
  • We keep controls, links, and metadata consistent across updates
  • When a page is no longer high quality, it is removed from the curated index

3. Updates and maintenance

We periodically review indexed pages for broken links, outdated guidance, and policy consistency. Pages that no longer meet quality standards may be moved to non-indexed archive status until they are improved.

4. Advertising and editorial independence

Advertising does not control game rankings or review notes. Game inclusion decisions are based on player usefulness and quality standards, not ad relationships.

5. Corrections and contact

If you see inaccurate information, broken links, or content concerns, contact us at hello@pxlrush.com. We review correction requests and update pages when needed.
