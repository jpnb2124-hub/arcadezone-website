Sudoku

Category: puzzle • Tag: Puzzle

Sudoku is strongest when played methodically. Fast guesses feel productive, but disciplined elimination solves puzzles more reliably.

How to approach this game

Scan rows, columns, and boxes for singles first, then move to candidate pairs and hidden constraints. Keep notes tidy so advanced patterns stay visible.

Common mistakes to avoid

The usual trap is placing numbers on assumption instead of proof. One bad guess can corrupt half the board.

Who this game is best for

Sudoku suits players who enjoy pure logic and steady progression from easy warmups to harder grids.
